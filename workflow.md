# Workflow Logic

1. A new lead is discovered.
2. Company information is collected.
3. The system checks whether the company was contacted in the last 30 days.
4. If the company is new, the lead is added to Google Sheets.
5. AI generates a personalized outreach message.
6. A human reviews the message.
7. The email is opened in Outlook and manually sent.
8. The sent date and status are updated.
9. A follow-up is prepared after several days if needed.
